# Code of conduct

## About this project

I built Nutria as a hobby: to learn, and to solve a problem I had planning my
own meals. I released it as open source in case it is useful to someone else.

Suggestions, bug reports and contributions are welcome, but I work on Nutria
in my spare time and don't plan to invest a lot in it. In practice:

- Replies to issues and pull requests may take a while, and some may never
  come.
- I may decline a change that is good but falls outside what I want Nutria
  to be (see [`docs/vision.md`](docs/vision.md)), or that I can't maintain.
- There is no support, roadmap or release schedule. Forks are welcome.

None of this is a judgment on you or your work. It is just the time I have.

## How to behave

Treat everyone here the way you would want to be treated by a stranger who is
giving you their free time.

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Keep criticism about the code and the ideas, never about the person.
- Respect a "no", including mine on a change.
- Welcome people of every background, identity and level of experience.

Not acceptable, anywhere in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, and anything else tied to the repository):

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Pressuring the maintainer or other contributors with demands, deadlines or
  repeated pings.

## If something goes wrong

I'm one person, not a moderation team, and I won't investigate or mediate
disputes. If someone breaks these rules, report them to GitHub with its
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
tool: GitHub handles abuse on its platform.

When I see something that breaks these rules, I may hide or delete comments,
lock or close threads, or block someone from the repository, without warning
or discussion. That's all the enforcement there is.
